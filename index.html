clack school

Learn to type without looking, one or two keys at a time. Each lesson takes 2–4 minutes. Accuracy first — speed comes on its own.

⌨ keyboard feature. Clack School teaches 10-finger typing on a physical keyboard, so it won't work with a phone's on-screen keyboard. Open clack on a computer — or connect a keyboard and press any key.

put your hands in place

  1. Sit up, relax your shoulders, and keep your wrists just above the keyboard.
  2. Rest your left fingers on A S D F and your right fingers on J K L ; — this is the home row.
  3. Feel the little bumps on F and J. Your index fingers live there, so you can find home without looking.
  4. Rest your thumbs on the space bar. Keep your eyes on the screen from now on.
press space when you're ready

how it works
the basics

Pick a mode above START and just start typing — there's nothing to click into. A word advances the moment you type its last letter, right or wrong, so you never have to press Space. Backspace fixes a letter before the word ends, Space skips a word early, and Esc quits.

modes
⏱ time
Type as many words as you can in 15 to 180 seconds.
⁝ words
Race to finish a set of 10 to 100 words.
❝ quote
Type a real quote — short, medium or long. The author is shown when you finish.
✷ shoot
A typing shooter: blast falling words before they hit your base. See below.

Click the setting next to START (like 60 seconds ▾) to change the length, turn on ! punctuation, # numbers or ◎ weak keys for time and words runs, and switch sound or the light/dark theme.

clack school

New to typing? Clack School teaches you to type without looking, one or two keys at a time. Each lesson has four short steps — meet the keys (glowing fingertip pads on the keyboard show which finger to use and where it reaches), drill the motion, type words made only from keys you know, then a scored check. A wrong key doesn't move you on, and Clack tells you which finger you used by mistake. Forgot where your hands go? Press ✋ hand position under the keyboard to see the home position again (the clock pauses while you look).

Clack School needs a physical keyboard — on a phone's on-screen keyboard you type with your thumbs, so the lessons don't apply. Connect a keyboard (or open clack on a computer) and the lessons work as normal.

Pass a lesson with 90% accuracy to unlock the next one; higher accuracy and speed earn up to three stars. The on-screen keyboard fades out while you're typing accurately, so you learn not to look. You can pin it if you'd rather keep it.

daily challenge

Everyone gets the same 30 words each day. You get one official attempt — after that, the daily card on the home screen shows your score and a countdown to the next one, which unlocks at midnight. Play on consecutive days to build your daily streak.

Share your result as text (one square per word: 🟩 clean, 🟨 had a mistake), post it straight to 𝕏, or save a result card image.

leaderboard & your name

There are three boards: the daily, 30s and 60s (time runs without punctuation, numbers or weak keys). Each shows every player's best run, with separate keyboard and phone lists so thumbs never race ten fingers. The 30s and 60s boards can show today, this week or all time.

To join, pick a name — no email, no password. Clack gives you a recovery code instead: it's the key to your name, so save it somewhere safe. Enter it on another device to sign in there, and your stats, clacks, badges and store items sync between them. You can change your name once every 60 days, and a name that's unused for six months is freed for someone else.

Scores aren't taken on trust: your keystrokes and their timing are sent, and the server replays the run to work out the wpm itself. Nothing leaves your device until you pick a name, and you can delete your name and scores at any time.

weak-key practice

Clack tracks which keys you miss. Turn on ◎ weak keys, or press practise these keys on the stats page, and most of your words will be packed with the letters you fumble most. Once a key has enough data and a miss rate of 5% or more, it counts as weak. These runs don't count toward your personal bests, because the words are harder on purpose.

wpm & accuracy

WPM counts characters, not words. Every 5 correct keystrokes — spaces included — count as one "word". It's the standard typing-test measure, so long words and short words score fairly.

wpm = (correct characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes
e.g. 244 correct characters in 60 s → 244 ÷ 5 = 48.8 → 49 wpm, even if you typed only 40 actual words

Accuracy is correct characters ÷ all characters typed. The faint number behind the word is your live wpm, and combo ×N counts clean words in a row — one wrong letter resets it.

the shooter

Words fall toward your ship. Type a word's first letter to lock on — you'll target the lowest word starting with that letter — then finish it to destroy it. A wrong letter drops the lock. Every word that reaches your base costs a life; lose them all and it's game over. Each word is worth 10 + its length in points. calm, normal and frenzy change speed, spawn rate and lives, and everything speeds up the longer you survive.

power-ups

Power-ups fall as glowing capsules marked "power-up". Type the name to grab it. Missing one never costs a life.

❄ freeze
Slows every word — and new ones appearing — to a quarter speed for 5 seconds.
×2 double
Double points for 10 seconds. Grabbing freeze or double replaces the one running.
◈ shield
Gives back a lost life. At full health it puts a ring around your ship that blocks the next hit.
✹ boom
Saves a bomb. Press Space to clear every word on screen for full points.
progress

The heatmap on the home screen shows how much you typed each day, with your day streak and this month's totals. Detailed stats has your personal best for every mode, your wpm over time, and a keyboard that shows which keys you miss most.

Everything is saved in this browser, on this device. There are no accounts yet, so your progress doesn't follow you to another device, and clearing this site's data resets it.

clacks & the store

Clacks are Clack's currency. You earn them by playing: about 1 for every 25 correct characters in a typing run (runs need at least 15 seconds or 10 words), 1 per 20 shooter points, +20 for the daily with a bonus for your daily streak, +15 for a new personal best, and a one-off reward for every badge.

Spend them in the store on new shooter ships and bullet trails. A few items also need a badge before you can buy them. Everything is cosmetic — nothing you buy changes your score.
